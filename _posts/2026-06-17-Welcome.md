Welcome to the new home of The International Compositae Alliance (TICA)!

We're excited to launch our redesigned website, which has been developed in partnership with the Global Biodiversity Information Facility (GBIF). This new platform provides a modern, flexible foundation for sharing information about the world's largest family of flowering plants while creating new opportunities to connect biodiversity data from GBIF with the resources and activities of the Compositae research community.

This blog will serve as a place to share updates from across the community, including upcoming conferences and workshops, new research, highlights from _Capitulum_ and other recent publications, project announcements, and other news of interest. Our goal is to create a central hub where researchers, students, and anyone interested in Compositae can easily stay connected with the latest news and developments.

We're excited about what's ahead and look forward to growing this resource together. Be sure to check back regularly as we continue to expand the site and add new features and content.

Welcome, and thank you for being part of the TICA community!
